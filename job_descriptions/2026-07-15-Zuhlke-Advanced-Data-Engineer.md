## Role Summary
As an Advanced Data Engineer, you are a key player in designing, implementing, and maintaining
data pipelines. You work closely with **clients** to understand their data needs and realise
tailored solutions that drive business insights. Consultancy / client-facing delivery.

## How you'll make an impact (Responsibilities)
- Be a trusted advisor, guiding customers toward a successful technical solution to their data challenges
- Communicate the "what", "why" and "how" of proposed solutions to technical and non-technical stakeholders
- Develop, test, and monitor distributed data processing pipelines
- Extrapolate versatile data sets and sources to produce high-quality, reproducible datasets in a scalable and maintainable way
- Collaborate with other data roles: Architects, Software Engineers, Data Scientists
- Understand the needs of many producers and consumers of data services; ensure products meet requirements
- Deliver projects in an Agile way, building iteratively to produce value from data early and frequently
- Stay technically sharp; open to learning new concepts and technologies

## What's important to us (Requirements)
- University degree (ETH, Uni, FH) in computer science, software engineering, data science, or comparable
- At least **3 years** in data or software engineering positions
- Experience designing, building, maintaining data products that meet the needs of data consumers
- Understanding of data analysis, data visualization, and optionally data science
- Experience with a variety of data architectures (Data Lake, Data Lakehouse, Medallion, Data Products, streaming, batch)
- Experience with Cloud Data Platforms: **Databricks, Snowflake, Microsoft Fabric, or Amazon Sagemaker**
- Practical data programming in **Python and SQL**
- Hands-on skills or keen interest in: **Apache Spark, Delta Lake, Airflow, Kafka, Kubernetes, Terraform, FastAPI**, and languages (Scala, R, Java, Typescript, .NET)
- Hands-on experience with both relational and non-relational databases
- Familiarity with big data infrastructures and concepts for large/heterogeneous data volumes
- Practical knowledge of varied structured and unstructured data (text, tabular, graph, time-series, geospatial, image)
- Experience with agile development and DevOps methodologies
- **Fluency in both German and English is required**
