## Role Summary
A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) on data42, the Novartis platform built on Palantir Foundry.
The role is embedded, user-facing technical delivery: sit with domain teams, understand their
workflows, and ship end-to-end solutions on Foundry.

## Responsibilities
- Embed with domain teams to understand workflows, define success criteria, and translate needs
  into shipped capabilities
- Design end-to-end solutions on Palantir Foundry: pipelines, transformations, and applications
- Data ingestion, modeling, and quality assurance across diverse datasets
- Build user-facing applications and integrate with upstream/downstream systems
- Collaborate cross-functionally with product, data science, governance, and platform teams
- Create reusable templates and mentor teammates

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Biology, Bioengineering, Bioinformatics, Biostatistics, or related field
- 8+ years in biomedical research, bioengineering, bioinformatics, or equivalent
- Hands-on experience with Palantir Foundry building and operationalizing solutions
- Experience partnering with stakeholders to define solutions and develop POCs
- Track record delivering software/data products in fast-paced, user-facing environments
- Strong programming skills across data and application layers
- Strong user empathy and workflow orientation, with direct end-user engagement
- Excellent communication and stakeholder management

## Desirable Qualifications
- Experience in regulated or sensitive-data environments with access-control expertise
- Product discovery methodology familiarity
- Analytics, AI, or machine-learning enablement experience

## Tech Stack
