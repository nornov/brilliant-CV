## 🏆 Strategic Positioning

### Your Top 3 Selling Points

1. **Agentic AI that ships, with guardrails**: The Jira-to-pipeline multi-agent workflow with quality contracts between subagents is exactly the "reusable capability" and "best practices in AI software engineering" story Siemens wants to scale across factories. Lead with it.
2. **Azure + Databricks production depth**: Certified Azure and Databricks engineer who runs production pipelines and cut cloud cost by €470k/year. Shows the architecture holds up at scale, not just in a demo.
3. **Hardware and industrial background**: 5 years on automotive electrical systems and EV power electronics, including at Valeo Siemens eAutomotive (a former Siemens joint venture). Easier to talk to OT and plant engineers than a pure software profile.

### Critical Gaps & Mitigation Strategy

- **Gap:** 4–5+ years at senior level
  **Impact:** Medium
  **Strategy:** Present ~4 years in AI/data engineering plus 5 years of engineering as one continuous technical track. Point to architecture ownership (agentic workflow design, team standards) rather than title. Do not claim "senior" years you don't have.
- **Gap:** Shop-floor OT (PLC, OPC UA, MES, edge deployment)
  **Impact:** Medium
  **Strategy:** Bridge with IoT telemetry ingestion and commissioning experience. Say plainly in the interview that factory OT protocols would be new, and show how quickly you learned Foundry/Databricks.
- **Gap:** LangGraph
  **Impact:** Low
  **Strategy:** You built multi-agent orchestration yourself; picking up LangGraph is a small step. Can be closed with a short side project before the interview.
